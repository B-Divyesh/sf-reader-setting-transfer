How the extension works

Apply one reading card to every article.

Your saved reading card sets the article view and lists each active value.

  1. 01

    Make your reading card

    Choose text size, line length, spacing, letter shapes, contrast, and reduced motion. See every change in a live preview.

  2. 02

    Open a public article

    Select the extension on a public article. It keeps headings, lists, quotes, tables, and links—without the surrounding clutter.

  3. 03

    Adjust or turn off the reader

    See the active reading card, adjust it, or turn the reader off for that site and return to the original page.

Export and import your reading card

Review every saved reading setting.

Export your reading card as a text file and import it in another browser. The file uses JSON and contains settings, not browsing history.

  • Visible values, not vague presets
  • Import on another browser
  • Turn off the reader on a site if an article looks wrong

What stays on your device

The extension does not send us your reading history.

Stored locallyYour reading card, site choices, and current article stay in browser extension storage.

Sent nowhereNo account, tracking, remote fonts, or server upload.

Easy to clearRemove the extension and its local data leaves with it.

Install the v1 package

Install the extension, then open it on each article.

  1. Download and unzip the extension package.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome or Chromium.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, choose “Load unpacked”, and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin Reader Setting Transfer and open it on a public article.
