Privacy, in plain language.

Reader Setting Transfer is designed so we do not receive your reading data.

What stays on your device

The extension stores your reading card, site choices, and latest article in local extension storage.

It uses this data only for the reader and does not send it to us.

What this website collects

This website has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, account system, or remote fonts.

Infrastructure logs may briefly record an IP address, browser details, requested URL, and time for security and reliability.

Permissions

The extension requests storage, active-tab, and scripting permissions.

It reads article text only after you open the extension and select Read this article.

It does not read or store pages while you browse without opening the extension.

Control and deletion

Export your reading card from the settings screen at any time.

Removing the extension deletes its local storage through your browser. You can also replace site choices in the extension settings.

Children and sensitive data

The extension does not collect account or article data. The website may process the limited infrastructure logs described above, including when a child visits it.

Do not place confidential information in reading card names.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be published here with a new effective date. Questions can be raised through the project’s public GitHub issue tracker.
